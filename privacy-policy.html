Idle Aces: Carrier Command: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Developer: Wolf Well Ink · Contact: gtveldel@gmail.com

Idle Aces: Carrier Command ("the game") is an offline-first idle strategy game for Android. This policy explains what data the game and the Google services inside it handle.

Short version

  • No accounts. The game has no sign-in and never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • The game is rated Teen and is not directed at children under 13.

1. Data saved on your device

Game progress and settings (stage, upgrades, crew, options, a list of purchase receipt tokens used to stop double grants) are stored in a local save file on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. We run no servers that hold player data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a bonus) and, occasionally, full-screen interstitial ads between waves. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off interstitials; rewarded ads stay available because they are opt-in.

Google AdMob may collect and process, according to Google's policies:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • ad interaction data (impressions, taps), and crash/diagnostic data from the ads SDK.

This data is used for showing ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention and, if you consent, personalized ads. Ad requests are marked for teen-appropriate content (max rating T) and not as child-directed.

Consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required), the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (Nova packs, Remove Ads, Starter Pack, permanent x2 boosts) are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment information under its own privacy policy. The game receives only the purchase details it needs (product id, purchase token, purchase state) to deliver the item and to restore non-consumable items via Settings → Restore Purchases. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • We have no user accounts, chat or social features.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over (Teen). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected through the ads SDK, contact us and we will help.

6. Data retention and deletion

We hold no player data on our side. Local save data stays on your device until you delete it (uninstall or clear app data). Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies; you can manage it at https://myaccount.google.com and in your device's ad settings.

7. Security

Network traffic from the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
